Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026.

This page explains what Apex Exteriors collects when you use this website, why, and how to have it deleted. If you just want the short version: we collect what you type into our forms so we can call you back about a free estimate. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone outside the vendors that keep this site running.

What the estimate form collects

The forms on our homepage and hero section ask for your name, phone number, address, and what service you're interested in. Email is optional where we ask for it. That information is sent to us through Netlify Forms and used only to follow up about your request.

What the storm history lookup collects

Our storm history tool lets you type in an address to see hail and wind events recorded nearby over the last ten years. When you run a lookup, we record the address you typed and a short summary of what the search turned up, so we can follow up about a free inspection.

We do not store the coordinate returned by the map service — only the address text you entered. The map itself is provided by Mapbox, which processes the address you type in order to locate it on the map; see Mapbox's privacy policy for how they handle that.

Where it goes

Form submissions are processed by Netlify Forms and delivered to us by email. We don't run our own server or database — Netlify is the only third party that handles your submitted information on our behalf, alongside Mapbox for the map and address lookup described above.

What we use it for

  • Calling or texting you back about a free estimate or inspection
  • Understanding which parts of the Mid-South we're getting inquiries from
  • Nothing else — we don't run ads or sell leads

Who we share it with

We don't sell, rent, or trade your information. It's used internally by Apex Exteriors to follow up on your request, full stop.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics pixels. Netlify's form handling includes basic spam filtering, and Mapbox may set functional cookies required to serve map tiles.

How to have your information deleted

Call us at (901) 494-7506 and ask, and we'll remove your information from our records.

Children

This site isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

If this policy changes

We'll update the date at the top of this page if anything here changes in a way that affects what we collect or how we use it.

Questions

Call us at (901) 494-7506 with any questions about this policy or your information.
